About

I am currently a third-year Ph.D. student in CS at Georgia Tech co-advised by Prof. Ahmed Saeed and Prof. Alexandros Daglis. I completed my M.S. in ECE at Georgia Tech. Before joining Georgia Tech, I completed my B.S. in CS at Peking University.

My research interests lie in networked system, computer architecture, and computer networks. I am currently researching on resource-aware heterogeneous microservice request load balancing. In my M.S., I worked on project about design automation for nanomodular electronics. In my B.S., I worked on project about kernel code optimization for tensor operators.
